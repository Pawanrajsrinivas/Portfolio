About

Pawan Raj Srinivas is a filmmaker, director, writer and editor based in Bengaluru, India, creating short films, documentaries and visual stories.

His creative work explores storytelling through directing, cinematography, editing and screenplay writing, with a focus on bringing ideas from the first frame to the final cut.

This portfolio brings together his filmmaking projects, short films, documentaries, animation and other visual work.
